# Code of conduct

MxTac follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies to the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions and any other space where the project is represented.

In short:

- Be respectful. Disagree about code and ideas, not about people.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Do not publish other people's private information.
- Assume good intent, and accept correction when you get something wrong.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainers privately through the repository's [security advisory form](https://github.com/fankh/mxtac/security/advisories/new), marked "Code of conduct". Reports are kept confidential. Do not use a public issue for this.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor. The enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant apply.
